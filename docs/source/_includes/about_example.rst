.. admonition:: About this example

    A minimal example: it shows how nodes compose into a pipeline that does
    something real, in a few minutes. Its limits are deliberate: it needs no
    GPU and uses small models, and has no guardrails or hardening, so its
    quality and latency are not those of a tuned deployment. To adapt it,
    follow the :ref:`guides <howto>`; to extend it, pick nodes from the
    :doc:`node catalogue </catalog/nodes/overview>`; to take it to
    production, with tuned performance, hardware sized to the workload and
    scaling, :doc:`work with Meetecho </work_with_meetecho>`.
